Rotation Detection Microswitch .

Types A & B are fully interchangeable.

Can be used in either clockwise and counter clockwise positions.

F or all Tomsed & Boon Edam Waist High Turnstile Models

For both Full Height and Waist High Alvarado Turnstiles as well.

Type A is a 2 part assembly of microswitch body with the add-on one-way roller accessory. Please note that Type A is is subject to more frequent failure due to the flexing and metal fatigue of the actuator arm.

Type B is a one piece switch with integral roller actuator. The actuator arm for Type B rocks rather than flexes and as such has a longer service life.

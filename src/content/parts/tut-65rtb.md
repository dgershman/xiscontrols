Tomsed Model TUT-65RTB was designed for use in Stadiums and Arenas so they are built for tough usage. These have an integral ticket box, Hip Knob/Hand Control which activates the blocking mechanism, rear mounted wheels for portability and optional mechanical counter to register passages.

Internally, the mechanism is similar to the TUT-60 security turnstile and as such, share many of the same parts and robust construction. These units sold originally for around $3,500 - $4,000 each. Completely recertified and upgraded by our Tomsed trained technicians.

Looking for a solution to the one-way pedestrian traffic problem for your business? These turnstiles are the perfect answer. Place one at your entrance and even one at your exit to permit entry or exit only. There is even a place for a message placard for advertising or information.

Where there is need for electrical control, either from a card reader or remote switch, we can upgrade the mechanism for a Fail-lock entry.

Pre 2003 Locking Arms have been discontinued. XIS Controls continues to supply a replacement kit by adapting the "LA" arms for use in the older model MST turnstiles and has assigned the suffix "LP"

The Kit consists of: One Left Lock Arm, part number 04-2040-Alt. One Right Lock Arm, part number 04-2050-Alt. and Two 11-1661 Shoulder Bolts.

This older style does not use thrust washers

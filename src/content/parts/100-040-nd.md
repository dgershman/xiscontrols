Teflon Thrust Disc for Tomsed THT-100 All Mechanical/Non Electric Full Height Turnstiles

Non-Electric, Mechanical only Tomsed THT-100 Full Height Turnstiles does not employ the Thrust Bearing 100-040 above. Rather they use a Nylon/Teflon Thrust Disc as the bearing surface. Do not attempt to use this disc as a lower cost alternative for electric turnstiles as you will damage the floor plate and lower flange.

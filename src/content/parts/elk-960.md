General purpose timer relay. Operates on 12 to 24 VDC. A quick jumper setting converts the time from seconds to minutes. The timer can be configured to activate once for each trigger, or free-run as soon as power is applied. Relay can be configured to be normally on or normally off. Also used in our Serial Port Interface TX9-TMR/2

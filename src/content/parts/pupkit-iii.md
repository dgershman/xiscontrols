PUPKIT III+

B ackwards Compatible. Uses the new TCB 6.4 RED Version Boards. Adds Microswitch Fault Diagnostics. Metal Detector Lockout and All Release functions are included.

Upgrade Controller for Robot, Philips, Burle & Dearborn Full Height Turnstiles which includes STG, CSTG and ASTG Turnstile Models.

This upgrade kit completely replaces any relays, FP0 type PLCs, and power supply that your present turnstile has installed. A new life for your turnstile. Installation is straight forward and simple. Helps extend the life of your solenoids by providing them the proper operating voltage.

The original PUPKIT is Designed and Manufactured only by XIS Controls

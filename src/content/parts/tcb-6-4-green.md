Fully backwards compatible with all prior versions: TCB 1, 2, MCB 3, 4, 5, TCB 6x. Increased Sensitivity and Access Granted and Rotation Reset Test Pushbuttons. Configurable One-Shot Pulse Option. Can replace Aegis Mectronics & DSI Control Boards too. We keep re-designing because our customers want new features.

This board is a combination in a single board of the former 30-001, 30-002, & 30-003

Used in turnstile series models: TUT-50, TUT-60, TUT-65, TST-75, THT-100 and in the PUPKIT

Like it's predecessors in the 6.X series, will be shipped in the Tomsed/Boon Edam Configuration and allow re-configuration for use in the PUPKIT

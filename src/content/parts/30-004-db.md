We have introduced an improved version of the 30-004 bearing.

The factory supplied bearings have no side shields leaving exposed bearing balls subject to contamination, is constructed from ordinary steel and is a custom one-of-a-kind design.

Our bearing design uses off the shelf bearings that are lubricated, shielded, and are made of polished chrome steel and less subject to rusting. We use 2 individual bearings to replace the dual row bearing factory supplied design. All these factors help minimize the likelihood of a stalled bearing which will cause drag and destructive wear on the nylon or urethane centering disc.

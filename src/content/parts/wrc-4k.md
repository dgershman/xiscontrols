No need to have a desk mounted "buzz to enter" button. Operates 4 turnstiles, gates or doors remotely.

Form "C" relay output -10 amp. Maximum. 100' operating range depending on obstructions. Pushbutton/jumper programmable: Momentary, On/Off, Interlock. Can learn up to 12 remotes. Comes with snap-on cover for the relay board.

Want to control 4 items with your Smart Phone? We recommend mating this WRC-4K with a Sonoff Bridge WiFi and the eWeLink App.

Two-way Roller Long Lever Microswitch used in most Tomsed & Boon Edam Full Height Turnstiles as the solenoid activation switch. Can be found in various other models as a special application microswitch. These include the TST-75DA (Drop Arm), the THT-100MT (Man Trap) and one-way only full height models..

Often incorrectly listed as 101-013E. This is the part number for the switch mounting bracket when used as a solenoid activation switch.

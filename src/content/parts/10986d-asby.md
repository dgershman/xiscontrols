BOON EDAM 10986 Solenoid Replacement AKA Pontiac J1203A

Almost without exception Boon Edam Full Height Turnstiles with the "U" shaped solenoid mounting bracket were supplied with a non-standard Pontiac J1203A solenoid.

These Pontiac Coil Solenoids used by Boon Edam have a Nonstandard long 7/8" diameter bushing thread. This is Not an Industry Standard forcing you to keep coming back to them for replacements.

A 1" Diameter Bushing Thread is a standard in the industry and we have put together a functionally superior replacement kit. The kit includes our replacement solenoid (Deltrol and Guardian) with extension collar and New "U" Shaped Mounting Bracket to accommodate the 1" Bushing.

Fail-Lock and Fail-safe configurations available. (spring and linkage - not included)

Deltrol (10986D) is a High Power solenoid for use with Fail-Lock configurations only.

Guardian (10986G) is a Medium Power solenoid for either Fail-Lock or Fail-safe configurations.

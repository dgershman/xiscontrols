Replacement Solenoids for all Tomsed and some Boon Edam 24VDC Full Height Turnstiles.

Part 101-008 will replace LEDEX/SAIA-BURGESS 192917-00x , and PONTIAC J1203A Solenoids which are installed with the above pictured 2 Bracket system . "L" shaped Solenoid bracket and separate"L" shaped Stroke Limit Bracket. If your stroke limit bracket is missing, we have them.

If your turnstile has the single "U" shaped solenoid bracket installed, the 101-008 will not work. See part 10986 below.

Perey Full Height Roto-Gate Turnstiles employed a 110VAC Namco series solenoid. These solenoids are no longer manufactured and the only solution, other than replace your turnstile, is to retrofit yours with another configuration, power/stroke equivalent solenoid. Roto-Gate customers have successfully performed this retrofit of their existing RG-560 Solenoid.

Perey also used these solenoids in their later production run of the Roto-Gate
